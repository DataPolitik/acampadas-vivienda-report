# Housing camps on Telegram, Spain, autumn 2026

A study of the public Telegram channels of the Spanish housing movement and their
allies during the housing camps that followed the eviction of Maricarmen Abascal in
Madrid, and during the general strike for housing that followed: how the channels were
sampled and classified, and what their forwarding and mention networks show.

## First study: main findings

- **Observation window:** 14 September to 5 October 2026
- **Sample:** 167 public channels — 24 housing organisations, 91 allies, 50 media
- **Data:** 22,111 messages scanned, 2,403 forwards, 2,049 mentions and links to other accounts

1. **Housing channels are sources, not relays.** Allies forwarded housing content 156
   times; housing channels forwarded ally content 12 times.
2. **One hub.** The Sindicato de Inquilinas de Madrid is forwarded by 14 channels across
   Madrid, València and Andalusia; no other housing channel is relayed by more than 7.
3. **Forwarding starts with the eviction.** Forwards of housing channels' posts go from
   0–2 a day before 23 September to 20 that day, and peak at 35 a day.
4. **No single housing community.** The network clusters by territory and political
   current. Territorial housing clusters (Madrid, València, Málaga, the PAH) do not
   forward each other.
5. **The camp channels are not forwarded by anyone;** they appear only through mentions.

## Follow-up: main findings so far

1. **The Madrid union stops being the hub.** It is forwarded by 10 channels in the two
   weeks after Maricarmen's death, then by 1 and 2 in the following weeks.
2. **Málaga's strike committee channel carries the forwarding of housing content,** but
   through the same three or four channels: volume without breadth.
3. **The mobilisation turns from the camps to the 11 November strike,** and channels that
   back the strike while disputing its organisers grow from 5 to 13 in a week.
4. **Territorial clusters still do not forward each other;** the first tie across
   territories is a joint statement by the camps after the Urda rail crash.
5. **Two hostile media channels form a closed pair** that no other channel forwards or
   quotes.

## About this site

- Only public Telegram channels and groups were read, without joining them.
- The reports are published as written for the project, with internal file references
  removed from the follow-up's pages. They refer to scripts, data files and Gephi graphs
  that are **not** published here; only the documents listed above and the figures are.
- In the evidence files, individual user accounts and hidden-name forward origins are
  pseudonymised, and the text of members' messages in group chats is removed. Channels
  are identified by their public username.
- The sample is a snowball from a nine-channel seed, not a census. See the limitations
  sections before drawing conclusions.
